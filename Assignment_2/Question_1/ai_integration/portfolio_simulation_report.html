Methodology

Parameters are estimated from the full actual sample. Each repetition draws T multivariate-normal industry returns using the estimated mean vector and covariance matrix. Unconstrained MVP and tangency weights are computed from the simulated sample and normalized to sum to one. The tangency portfolio uses the full-sample average monthly risk-free rate. Each weight vector is then applied to the original—not simulated—industry-return matrix. Reported means and standard deviations are monthly and use sample standard deviation (ddof=1).
